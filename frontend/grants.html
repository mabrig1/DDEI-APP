Before you apply — read this

  • Always confirm on the official page. Grant rounds open and close constantly. We date every listing so you can see how fresh it is.
  • A listing marked "unconfirmed" means exactly that. The programme is real, but at least one detail — usually an amount or a deadline — could not be checked against an official source. We show you which.
  • No legitimate grant asks you to pay to apply. If a "grant" wants an application fee, processing fee or your bank details up front, it is a scam. Every listing here applies through the organisation's own website.
